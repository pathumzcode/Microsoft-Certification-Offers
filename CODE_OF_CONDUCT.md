# Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this community a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on the work and the evidence, not on the person.
- Accept good-faith corrections and differing perspectives.
- Share only information you are authorized to disclose.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, unwelcome sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post passwords, private learner information, voucher codes, or other secrets.

## Applying these standards

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary. Report a conduct concern privately to the repository maintainer using the contact or private reporting method published on the repository hosting page. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

This community policy is intended to support respectful participation; it does not replace the terms of the services linked from this guide.

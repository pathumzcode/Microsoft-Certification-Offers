# 01. Programme overview

## Purpose

This guide explains how LevelUp certification preparation relates to a specific exam-voucher promotion. LevelUp learning access and a Microsoft exam voucher are separate things: the first does not automatically provide the second.

## Prerequisites

- A current, offer-eligible LevelUp account and registered email.
- The official voucher terms for the offer you intend to use.
- A course that the terms list for that offer.

## How the stages fit together

1. **Account:** Create or access your LevelUp learner account. This only establishes platform access.
2. **Enrolment:** Find a course listed under the offer and enrol. Enrolment is not proof that the offer accepts your account.
3. **Learning:** Complete the required “Welcome to cert prep” module and any course requirements indicated by the live offer.
4. **Assessment:** The current general terms require an 80% or higher practice-test result and allow one attempt under each offer.
5. **Eligibility:** The current terms require an active, Microsoft-approved MAICPP partner-domain registered email and impose offer-specific and other restrictions.
6. **Voucher decision:** Stock is limited, partner-domain allocations apply, and Microsoft approval is required. A course or passing score alone does not guarantee issuance.
7. **Exam booking:** If a voucher is issued, redeem it according to its instructions and the current Microsoft/Pearson VUE scheduling rules.

Offer status, dates, courses, assessment conditions, and voucher scope are specific to the current offer. See [eligibility and requirements](02-eligibility-and-requirements.md) for the dated offer snapshot and confirm all details in the live [official terms](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/cert-terms-and-conditions).

## Expected result

You should understand which stage you have reached and what still must happen. In particular, a learner can have an account and complete a course without qualifying for or receiving a voucher.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

- **Treating all offers as interchangeable:** Use only the terms and eligible courses for the offer currently open.
- **Taking an assessment too early:** Read the one-attempt and required-module conditions before starting.
- **Expecting an automatic voucher:** Check the registered email after the stated processing window, but contact LevelUp support if needed; stock and approval still apply.
- **Assuming the award guarantees a specific exam booking:** Check the voucher's scope and Microsoft/Pearson VUE rules before scheduling.

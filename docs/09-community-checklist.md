# 09. Community checklist

Use this as a personal checklist, not as an eligibility determination. The current offer's official terms always take precedence.

## Before registration or enrolment

- [ ] Open the [current voucher terms](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/cert-terms-and-conditions).
- [ ] Confirm the intended offer is active and has not been declared closed.
- [ ] Confirm the course is listed under that offer, not only under an upcoming or expired offer.
- [ ] Understand the registered partner-domain email condition and any exclusions.
- [ ] Confirm you are comfortable with the one-attempt rule before starting the assessment.

## Learning and assessment

- [ ] Register or sign in to LevelUp using the official site.
- [ ] Enrol in the eligible course.
- [ ] Complete the required “Welcome to cert prep” module.
- [ ] Review the assessment instructions before beginning.
- [ ] Take the one allowed practice-test attempt only when prepared.
- [ ] If an error occurs, contact official support before retrying.

## After the assessment

- [ ] Keep your own private note of course, result, and completion date.
- [ ] Check inbox and spam/junk over the stated 5–7 business-day window.
- [ ] Remember that course completion and a passing score do not guarantee a voucher.
- [ ] If a voucher is received, read all instructions and note the 60-day redemption requirement.
- [ ] Confirm the exam and voucher type match before scheduling.
- [ ] Verify the final price, candidate name, exam date, and provider policies before confirming a booking.
- [ ] Keep the voucher code, account details, and booking confirmation private.

## Screenshot reference

For public-page images and pending learner-only captures, see the [screenshot checklist](../screenshots/README.md) and [manifest](../screenshots/manifest.md). Do not treat this checklist as evidence of account eligibility or voucher award.

## Unverified

This checklist does not check live offer status, remaining voucher stock, your account, partner affiliation, or an exam provider's current appointments. Verify all of those directly with official services.

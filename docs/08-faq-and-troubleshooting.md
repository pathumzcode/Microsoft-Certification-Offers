# 08. FAQ and troubleshooting

## Purpose

Answer common questions using the official LevelUp FAQ and voucher terms checked on **2026-10-16**. Offer conditions may change; recheck official sources before acting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is LevelUp account registration enough to qualify?

No. Registration provides platform access only. See [eligibility and requirements](02-eligibility-and-requirements.md) for the offer conditions.

### Can someone using a personal email qualify?

Use the [eligibility guide](02-eligibility-and-requirements.md) and [account guide](03-account-registration.md), then confirm the live terms. Ask LevelUp support how to handle an existing account associated with a personal address.

### Do I need a Partner One ID?

Requirements can depend on the account and offer. Check the live registration instructions and [official FAQ](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/faqs), or contact LevelUp support; do not post your ID publicly.

### Which courses qualify?

Only the courses listed in the current terms for that offer. See the dated [course list and offer snapshot](02-eligibility-and-requirements.md), then verify the live terms.

### What must I complete?

Requirements vary by offer. See [learning modules and practice assessment](05-learning-modules-and-practice-assessment.md) and verify the current terms before starting.

### Is a voucher guaranteed if I pass?

No. See [eligibility and requirements](02-eligibility-and-requirements.md) and [voucher claiming and delivery](06-voucher-claiming-and-delivery.md).

### When should I receive a voucher?

Follow [voucher claiming and delivery](06-voucher-claiming-and-delivery.md) for the stated delivery window and support steps; check the latest terms.

### When does the voucher expire?

Check the deadline in the current terms and the instructions that accompany your voucher. See [voucher claiming and delivery](06-voucher-claiming-and-delivery.md) for the unresolved exam-date interpretation.

### Can Microsoft employees or v-dash vendors qualify?

See the [offer-specific exclusions](02-eligibility-and-requirements.md) and confirm that they still apply in the live terms.

### Is the voucher good for a Fundamentals exam?

Check the exact exam and voucher type in the live terms and your voucher instructions before booking; see [exam redemption](07-microsoft-exam-redemption.md).

### Where should I report a course or voucher problem?

Use the official [LevelUp Helpdesk](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/support). The terms direct invalid voucher and course-content issues there. For a technical lab issue, the terms list `support@skillable.com`.

## Troubleshooting checklist

1. Confirm you are on the official LevelUp or Microsoft page and not a look-alike.
2. Record the page, course code, approximate time, and visible error for your private support request.
3. Remove passwords, verification codes, personal email addresses, account IDs, and voucher codes from screenshots before sharing.
4. For browser loading issues, the official LevelUp FAQ recommends the latest Edge, Chrome, Firefox, or Safari; clearing the browser cache; turning off VPN/proxy; or trying an InPrivate window.
5. For unresolved registration, enrolment, course, or voucher issues, contact the official LevelUp Helpdesk.

## Unverified

The FAQ does not describe this voucher offer in detail. Voucher-specific answers above come from the terms, not from assumptions about general account behavior. Live stock, offer closure, individual account eligibility, and voucher-specific deadline interpretation require official confirmation.

# 11. Complete course walkthrough route map

Use this route map to follow the process in order. The detailed guides below are the source of truth for instructions and screenshots; each screenshot is kept with the step it illustrates rather than duplicated in this overview.

> **Important:** Platform access, course enrolment, course completion, voucher eligibility, voucher issuance, and exam redemption are separate stages. Completing a course does not guarantee a voucher.

1. Check the current offer rules and course list in [eligibility and requirements](02-eligibility-and-requirements.md).
2. [Register or sign in](03-account-registration.md) to LevelUp.
3. Find an eligible course and [enrol](04-finding-and-enrolling-in-courses.md).
4. Complete the course's required learning and [practice assessment](05-learning-modules-and-practice-assessment.md).
5. Check the rules and next steps for [voucher claiming and delivery](06-voucher-claiming-and-delivery.md).
6. If you receive a voucher, follow [Microsoft exam redemption and scheduling](07-microsoft-exam-redemption.md).

Public screenshots are shown beside the relevant actions in those guides. Screens that need an authorized account or contain private information remain marked pending in the [screenshot manifest](../screenshots/manifest.md); they must not be fabricated.

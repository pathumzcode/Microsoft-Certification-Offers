## Changes and unresolved questions

The current terms show a distinct active offer launched October 9, 2026, the required “Welcome to cert prep” module, an 80% minimum with one attempt, 5–7-business-day issuance, and a 60-day redemption condition. The page also distinguishes an upcoming Sales Certification Week offer and a closed Frontier Transformation Engineer offer. Compare the current terms directly before relying on any older guide or search result.

The following require manual confirmation from the live terms or official support:

- Whether the active offer still has stock and whether it has been closed.
- Whether the MAICPP partner list cited as of July 31, 2026 has changed.
- Whether the 60-day redemption deadline requires the candidate to sit the exam, or only redeem/schedule the voucher, by that date.
- Any location-specific conditions, voucher redemption countries, exam language/delivery restrictions, or further account/organization rules not spelled out in the terms.
- How LevelUp handles existing accounts registered with a personal email or attempts interrupted by technical errors.
- The final terms and start dates for the Sales Certification Week offer marked upcoming.
- Exact signed-in course progress indicators, lesson activity completion rules, assessment submission/result screens, and dashboard completion state.

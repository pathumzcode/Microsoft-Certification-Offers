# 02. Eligibility and requirements

## Purpose

Use this checklist before enrolling or taking the practice test. The official terms change by offer; the following snapshot reflects the terms checked on **2026-10-16** and must be rechecked before action.

## Prerequisites

- Read the [official voucher terms](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/cert-terms-and-conditions) in full.
- Confirm the desired offer is active and that the course is eligible for that exact offer.
- Confirm the email registered to your LevelUp learner account meets the domain rule.
- Understand that the current terms allow a single practice-test attempt per offer.

## Required conditions in the terms reviewed

1. **Offer timing:** A practice test passed before the offer start does not qualify under that offer.
2. **Registered email:** The registered email ID must be affiliated with an active Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (MAICPP) partner domain approved by Microsoft. The official partner list cited by the terms is dated July 31, 2026. A personal email address alone does not satisfy the stated partner-domain requirement.
3. **Course and module:** Complete the required module named “Welcome to cert prep” and the practice test associated with any one eligible course for that offer.
4. **Score and attempt:** Achieve at least 80% on the practice test. The terms permit one attempt to pass under each offer.
5. **One voucher per offer:** One exam voucher per learner per offer, even if multiple eligible courses are completed.
6. **Stock and approval:** Voucher availability is limited, allocations are capped per partner domain, and issuance is subject to stock and Microsoft approval.
7. **Exclusions:** Microsoft employees do not qualify for a LevelUp 100% discounted voucher. Microsoft vendors using a v-dash account are ineligible for a 100% discounted voucher under the stated offer.
8. **Exam scope:** The terms describe the voucher types as Microsoft Advanced Role-Based and state that these voucher types do not apply to Fundamentals certification exams.

LevelUp's general FAQ says registration uses a work email and that a valid Microsoft Partner One ID linked to the organization may be required for account registration/verification. This is general platform guidance; the voucher-specific partner-domain test is stated in the voucher terms. Contact LevelUp support if your organization or email affiliation is unclear.

## Eligibility check

Before enrolling or starting an assessment, compare the live offer's status, dates, partner-domain rule, course code, module, score, and attempt limit with your situation. Do not publish your email or Partner One ID for community verification. Meeting the published criteria still does not guarantee a voucher.

## Expected result

You should be able to tell whether you appear to meet the published requirements, but only LevelUp/Microsoft can make the official eligibility and allocation decision.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

- **Personal email only:** The offer terms require a registered email affiliated with the approved active partner domain. Ask support how to correct account details; do not create multiple accounts to work around a domain check.
- **Old or wrong course list:** Recheck Section 2 of the terms immediately before enrolling.
- **Multiple attempts:** Current terms say one practice-test attempt under each offer. Ask support before starting if the practice test fails to load or if you suspect a technical error; do not assume an attempt can be reset.
- **Course finished before offer start:** The terms exclude practice tests passed before the offer's start time.
- **Stock uncertainty:** Terms say “until stocks last” and the live remaining quantity is not published in the text reviewed. Ask LevelUp support if availability is unclear.
